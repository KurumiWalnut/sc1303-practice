Write `middle_node(head)` that returns the **middle node** of a non-empty linked list. If there are two middle nodes, return the **second** one.

### Examples

```text
middle_node(1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 5).data       →  3
middle_node(1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 5 -> 6).data  →  4    (second of the two middles)
```

### Constraints

- 1 to 100 001 nodes.
