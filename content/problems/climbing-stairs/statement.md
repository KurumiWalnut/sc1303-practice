You climb a staircase of `n` steps, taking **1 or 2** steps at a time. Write `climb_stairs(n)` that returns how many different ways you can reach the top.

### Examples

```text
climb_stairs(1)   →  1     (1)
climb_stairs(2)   →  2     (1+1, 2)
climb_stairs(3)   →  3     (1+1+1, 1+2, 2+1)
```

### Constraints

- `1 <= n <= 90`
