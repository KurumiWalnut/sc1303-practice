`numbers` is sorted in ascending order. Write `two_sum_sorted(numbers, target)` that returns the **1-based** positions `[i, j]` (with `i < j`) of the two numbers adding up to `target`. There's exactly one answer. Use O(1) extra space.

### Examples

```text
two_sum_sorted([2, 7, 11, 15], 9)   →  [1, 2]
two_sum_sorted([2, 3, 4], 6)        →  [1, 3]
two_sum_sorted([-1, 0], -1)         →  [1, 2]
```

### Constraints

- Up to 100 000 numbers.
