Numbers arrive one at a time. Write `running_medians(nums)` that returns a list whose `i`-th entry is the **median** of `nums[0..i]` as a float (the average of the two middle values when the count is even).

### Examples

```text
running_medians([5, 15, 1, 3])   →  [5.0, 10.0, 5.0, 4.0]
running_medians([2, 2])          →  [2.0, 2.0]
```

### Constraints

- Up to 3 000 numbers.
