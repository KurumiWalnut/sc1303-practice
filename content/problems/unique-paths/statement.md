A robot starts in the top-left corner of an `m` × `n` grid and can only move **right** or **down**. Write `unique_paths(m, n)` that returns how many different paths reach the bottom-right corner.

### Examples

```text
unique_paths(3, 7)   →  28
unique_paths(3, 2)   →  3     (right-down-down, down-right-down, down-down-right)
```

### Constraints

- `1 <= m, n <= 100`, and the answer is at most 2 × 10⁹.
