A **star** graph has one centre vertex joined to every other vertex, and no other edges. Vertices are numbered `1` to `n`. Given its `n - 1` edges, write `find_center(edges)` that returns the centre.

### Examples

```text
find_center([[1, 2], [2, 3], [4, 2]])            →  2
find_center([[1, 2], [5, 1], [1, 3], [1, 4]])    →  1
```
