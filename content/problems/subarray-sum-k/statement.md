Write `subarray_sum(nums, k)` that returns how many **contiguous** subarrays (runs of neighbouring elements) add up to exactly `k`. The numbers can be negative.

### Examples

```text
subarray_sum([1, 1, 1], 2)   →  2
subarray_sum([1, 2, 3], 3)   →  2     ([1, 2] and [3])
```

### Constraints

- Up to 20 000 numbers between -1000 and 1000.
