Repeatedly remove **two equal letters next to each other** from `s` until no such pair is left. Write `remove_duplicates(s)` that returns the final string (it's always the same, whatever order you remove pairs in).

### Examples

```text
remove_duplicates("abbaca")   →  "ca"     ("bb" goes, then "aa" goes)
remove_duplicates("azxxzy")   →  "ay"
```

### Constraints

- Up to 100 000 lowercase letters.
