Write `longest_common_subsequence(text1, text2)` that returns the length of the longest **subsequence** the two strings share (same order, letters may be skipped).

### Examples

```text
longest_common_subsequence("abcde", "ace")   →  3     ("ace")
longest_common_subsequence("abc", "abc")     →  3
longest_common_subsequence("abc", "def")     →  0
```

### Constraints

- `1 <= len(text1), len(text2) <= 1000`, lowercase letters.
