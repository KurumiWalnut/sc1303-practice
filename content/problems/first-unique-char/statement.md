Write `first_uniq_char(s)` that returns the **index** of the first character that appears **only once** in `s`, or `-1` if there isn't one.

### Examples

```text
first_uniq_char("leetcode")       →  0     ("l")
first_uniq_char("loveleetcode")   →  2     ("v")
first_uniq_char("aabb")           →  -1
first_uniq_char("swiss")          →  1     ("w")
```

### Constraints

- Up to 100 000 lowercase letters.
