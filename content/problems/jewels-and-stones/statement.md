Each letter in `jewels` is a kind of stone that's a jewel. Each letter in `stones` is a stone you have. Write `num_jewels_in_stones(jewels, stones)` that returns how many of your stones are jewels. Letters are case-sensitive: `"a"` and `"A"` are different.

### Examples

```text
num_jewels_in_stones("aA", "aAAbbbb")   →  3
num_jewels_in_stones("z", "ZZ")         →  0
```
