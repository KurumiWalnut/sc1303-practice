Write `max_sub_array(nums)` that returns the largest sum of a **non-empty contiguous** subarray.

### Examples

```text
max_sub_array([-2, 1, -3, 4, -1, 2, 1, -5, 4])   →  6     ([4, -1, 2, 1])
max_sub_array([1])                               →  1
max_sub_array([5, 4, -1, 7, 8])                  →  23
```

### Constraints

- Up to 100 000 values.
